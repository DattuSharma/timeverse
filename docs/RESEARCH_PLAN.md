# TIMEVERSE Research and Validation Plan

## Research question
Is there a sufficiently underserved user problem in watch research and ownership that TIMEVERSE can solve better than existing specialist databases, price trackers, identification apps, marketplaces and retailer comparison services?

Do not claim the broad concept is new. Existing services already cover overlapping capabilities.

## Competitor categories to audit
1. Watch identification and reference databases.
2. Watch specification and encyclopaedia platforms.
3. Secondary-market listings and price-history services.
4. Retailer/offer comparison and affiliate discovery.
5. Collection tracking and ownership management.
6. Watch servicing, warranty and provenance tools.
7. Watch education and media production tools.

Known starting examples from preliminary research include WatchCharts, EveryWatch, WatchID, FIND.WATCH, Chronex and FindWatch. Re-check current product status and exact features before relying on these notes.

## Audit template
For each competitor record:
- Product name and official URL.
- Last checked date.
- Primary audience and job-to-be-done.
- Search and exact-reference coverage.
- Specification provenance and correction process.
- Price coverage, geography, currency and update frequency.
- Collection, service and lifecycle features.
- Fit and comparison features.
- Media/AI features.
- Pricing and affiliate disclosures.
- Data sources, terms and licensing limitations.
- Strengths, gaps and evidence links.

## User discovery
Interview at least 10 potential users across first-time buyers, enthusiasts and owners. Ask about their last real watch research/purchase, information they could not verify, tools they used, and what they currently do after purchase. Avoid leading questions about the proposed solution.

## Validation gates
- Gate A: competitor map and prior-art review completed.
- Gate B: repeated user pain observed in interviews.
- Gate C: a narrow MVP job is selected and testable.
- Gate D: data sources and legal usage paths are identified.
- Gate E: prototype tested with users before expanding scope.

## Research integrity
Separate observed facts, vendor claims, user opinions and our hypotheses. Cite sources and dates. A missing competitor feature in a quick audit is not proof that it does not exist.
