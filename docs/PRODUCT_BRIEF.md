# TIMEVERSE Product Brief

## Vision
Build a trustworthy watch intelligence and ownership platform, beginning with an India-aware experience and an architecture that can expand internationally.

## Problem hypothesis
Watch information is fragmented across brand catalogues, retailer pages, enthusiast databases, market listings and service records. Buyers may struggle to identify the exact variant, compare dimensions, assess total ownership costs and distinguish reliable facts from repeated or conflicting claims.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven market gap.

## Target users
- First-time watch buyers who need clear explanations.
- Enthusiasts researching references, movements and specifications.
- Owners tracking warranty, maintenance and service history.
- Collectors comparing condition, provenance and resale information.

## Core user journey
1. Search by brand, model, reference or descriptive terms.
2. Select the exact reference/variant.
3. Review specifications with source evidence and confidence.
4. Compare candidates against a stated use case and budget.
5. Save a watch to a personal collection.
6. Record purchase, warranty, condition and service events.
7. Review ownership costs and available valuation evidence.

## Proposed differentiators to validate
- Evidence graph: claim-level citations, dates, confidence and conflicts.
- Lifecycle record: research through purchase, ownership, servicing and resale.
- Mission compiler: recommendations explained against user needs.
- Wrist-fit studio: dimension comparisons and clearly labelled approximations.
- Ownership cost model: purchase, upkeep, service and resale scenarios.
- Engineering microscope: understandable movement and component education.
- Cinema studio: licensed real imagery and separately labelled AI illustration.

These are product hypotheses. They are not claims of global uniqueness.

## MVP boundary
The first usable MVP should deliver:
- A polished responsive dashboard.
- Search across a curated, manually verified starter catalogue.
- Exact-reference detail pages.
- Specification evidence and source status.
- Side-by-side comparison.
- Saved collection.

Later phases add recommendations, fit estimates, lifecycle records, service journals, market insights and media creation.

## Out of scope for MVP
- Claims of AI authentication of physical watches.
- Unverified live market prices or price predictions.
- Unlicensed image/video scraping.
- Automatic buying/selling or payment handling.
- Expensive model inference as a required feature.

## Success criteria
- A user can find a catalogue watch and identify its exact reference.
- Every displayed factual specification has provenance or is explicitly marked unavailable/unverified.
- Comparisons use equivalent variants and units.
- Search, save and compare flows work end-to-end.
- Automated tests cover core API and data validation.
- The app runs locally with documented commands and no paid credentials.
